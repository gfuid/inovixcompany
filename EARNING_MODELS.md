### Where Ads Will Be Placed:
1. **16+ Free Tools (`/tools/*`):** Users file upload/process hone ke dauran 5–10 second screen par rukte hain — highest ad impression aur clicks yahan aate hain.
2. **Blog Articles (`/blog/*`):** Post ke beech me aur end me context-relevant tech ads.

### Ad Revenue Potential:

* **1,00,000 Page Views:** ₹20,000 – ₹45,000/mo
* **High-Tier Network (Ezoic/Mediavine at 50k+ visits):** 2x – 3x higher payout.
