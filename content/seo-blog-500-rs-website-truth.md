## Executive Summary: Can You Really Get a Business Website for ₹500?

No legitimate, revenue-generating business website can be built for ₹500. A standard `.com` or `.in` domain alone costs ₹700 to ₹1,200/year, and reliable cloud hosting costs ₹2,500 to ₹5,000/year. 

Advertisements offering *"Full Website in ₹500"* are bait-and-switch tactics used by amateur freelancers or fly-by-night operators. They use pirated, malware-infected WordPress templates that take 8+ seconds to load, hold your domain name hostage, generate zero Google rankings, and hit you with sudden ₹5,000–₹10,000 renewal fees.

## 1. The Anatomy of the "₹500 Website" Trap

Every week on Instagram, Facebook, and local classifieds in Haryana and across India, ads appear claiming:
- *"Get your business online in just ₹500!"*
- *"Free domain + hosting + 5-page website for ₹499 only!"*

For an unsuspecting shopkeeper, exporter, or clinic owner in Panipat, this sounds like an irresistible deal. But here is what actually happens when you transfer that ₹500:

### 1. Free Subdomains Instead of Your Own Brand
Instead of getting `yourbrand.com`, you are given a clumsy, untrusted URL like `yourbrand.freewebsitehost.site` or `yourbrand.wordpress.com`. No serious customer or corporate buyer trusts a company without its own branded domain.

### 2. Hostage Domains & Blackmail Renewals
If they do purchase a domain for you, they register it under **their own email**, not yours. When your business starts getting traction, they demand ₹8,000 to ₹15,000 to "renew" or release your domain. If you refuse, they shut down your site or redirect your traffic to your competitor.

### 3. Nulled Themes Injected with Malicious Code
To make a profit on ₹500, they download pirated ("nulled") themes from illicit forums. These files contain hidden PHP backdoors, spam injection scripts, and cryptocurrency miners that result in Google blacklisting your site within 30 days.

## 5. How to Protect Yourself: 4 Golden Rules Before Paying Any Agency

1. **Always purchase the domain yourself** (on Namecheap or Cloudflare) and grant DNS access, or ensure the registration receipt has your legal name and email.
2. **Demand a mobile PageSpeed audit** of past client websites. If their showcase websites score below 80, yours will too.
3. **Verify WhatsApp integration:** Ask how inquiries reach your phone. It should be instant, not buried in an unmonitored mailbox.
4. **Never accept nulled or pirated themes:** Ask if the code is custom React or a clean licensed theme.
