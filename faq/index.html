Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing, timelines, technology stacks, code ownership, and lead generation.

Why is Inovix so affordable compared to agencies charging ₹1,00,000+? +
Traditional agencies spend your hard-earned money on fancy air-conditioned offices, sales teams, and 5 layers of account managers who cannot even code. At Inovix, you work directly with the founder and senior engineers. We use modern, automated tech stacks that cut build time by 70%, passing 100% of the savings directly to you.
How long does it take to design and launch my website? +
Our Launchpad websites go live in just 3 to 5 business days. Full-scale multi-page web applications and custom CRMs typically take 10 to 18 days. You receive a private live development link on Day 1 to track progress in real time.
What is included in the ₹500/month hosting & maintenance plan? +
It includes ultra-fast cloud edge hosting, free SSL security, 99.9% uptime monitoring, automated weekly backups, and monthly content/photo/menu updates whenever you need them. Just message us on WhatsApp and we handle the technical side.
Do I get full ownership of my domain and source code? +
100% yes. You retain complete ownership of your domain, brand assets, and custom code repository. We never lock clients into hostage proprietary platforms, hidden contracts, or cancellation penalty fees.
How does Inovix ensure my website generates actual customer leads? +
We don't build digital brochures that sit idle. We build conversion funnels: high-contrast 1-click WhatsApp buttons, quick phone call triggers, Google Maps local profile integration, and instant inquiry forms that notify your phone the second a buyer clicks.
Can you build custom internal software, inventory, or CRM tools for my factory? +
Yes! We specialize in custom business tools, billing software, staff attendance portals, and export-import buyer catalog systems for Panipat manufacturers, exporters, and service businesses.
How does the milestone payment process work? +
We work on a transparent 50-50 milestone model: 50% advance to initiate architecture and staging link within 48 hours, and the remaining 50% only after you inspect and approve the final live deployment on your custom domain.
Can you migrate my existing slow WordPress or Hostinger site to React? +
Yes! We offer free zero-downtime migration. We re-engineer your slow, bloated WordPress site into modern sub-second React code, preserving your existing URLs, Google SEO rankings, and domain setup.
