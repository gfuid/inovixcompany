Website Design Company in Kundli, Sonipat: B2B Catalogue Websites for Manufacturers (2026)

Your buyers check you on Google before they call. Here is how a factory or B2B supplier in Kundli, Rai or Barhi should build a website that brings genuine enquiries.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

If you run a factory or trading unit and are looking for a website design company in Kundli, your need is different from a salon or a restaurant. You do not need fancy animation. You need a clean product catalogue, your company credentials, and an easy way for a purchase manager to send an enquiry. This guide is written for manufacturers and B2B suppliers in the Kundli, Rai and Barhi industrial areas of Sonipat.

1. Why a factory in Kundli, Rai or Barhi needs its own website

The Sonipat industrial belt sits right next to the Delhi border on the highway, with expressway connectivity around NCR. That location is why so many manufacturing, processing, packaging and engineering units operate here. Most of them get business through old relationships, dealers and B2B marketplaces.

The problem with depending only on marketplaces is simple: the same enquiry is sent to several suppliers, and the buyer picks the cheapest. Your own website works differently. When a buyer searches your company name or your product with "manufacturer in Sonipat", he lands on your page alone — your products, your certificates, your phone number. No competitor listing sits next to you.

A website also does quiet background work. Before placing a large order, most buyers and procurement teams check whether the supplier looks genuine. A proper website with address, GST details, product range and factory photos builds that trust before the first call.

2. What a B2B catalogue website must contain

  • Product catalogue with categories, clear photos, specifications, sizes, material and minimum order quantity where relevant.
  • Enquiry button on every product that opens WhatsApp or a short form with the product name already filled in.
  • About and infrastructure page: year of establishment, plant location, machinery, capacity and quality process — only real facts.
  • Certificates and registrations you actually hold, such as GST, MSME/Udyam, ISO or BIS, with copies if you are comfortable sharing them.
  • Downloadable PDF catalogue for buyers who want to forward it to their team.
  • Industries served and applications, so the buyer quickly sees if you fit his need.
  • Contact page with Google Maps location, phone, email and working hours.
  • Fast mobile loading — purchase managers also browse on phones.

What you do not need: sliders with ten banners, stock photos of foreign factories, or long paragraphs about "vision and mission". Buyers want specifications and a number to call.

3. Catalogue website vs full e-commerce

Most B2B suppliers do not need a shopping cart. Prices change with quantity, raw-material rates and credit terms, so an "Add to cart" button rarely fits. A catalogue-plus-enquiry website is simpler, cheaper and matches how B2B buying really happens. If you also sell small quantities directly to consumers, then online ordering can make sense — see our guide on e-commerce website development for that route.

5. Getting found: local SEO for industrial suppliers

A website alone is not enough; buyers must find it. For B2B suppliers the basics matter more than tricks:

  1. Google Business Profile with the correct factory address, category, photos and phone number, so you appear on Maps for searches around Kundli and Sonipat.
  2. One page per product category, written with the words buyers actually type, such as product name plus "manufacturer" or "supplier" and the location.
  3. Real photos of your products and plant instead of stock images.
  4. Consistent name, address and phone number everywhere online.

SEO takes time, and nobody can honestly guarantee a number one position. What we can promise is that the setup is done properly. Our Google Business Profile and local SEO guide explains the process in detail.
