WhatsApp Marketing Services in Panipat: Broadcasts, Catalog and Ads Without Bans

Bulk WhatsApp software looks cheap until your business number is banned. Here is the permitted way to run campaigns that people actually read.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Before every festival season, the same offer reaches business owners here: "1 lakh WhatsApp messages for a few thousand rupees". Many try it. Some get a few enquiries, many get their number blocked, and almost all annoy people who never asked to hear from them. Proper WhatsApp marketing services in Panipat work the opposite way: fewer messages, sent to people who agreed to receive them, through the official channel. This guide explains how that works and what to expect from it.

1. Bulk-sender software vs official WhatsApp marketing

This is the first decision, so let us settle it clearly.

PointUnofficial bulk-sender toolOfficial WhatsApp Business API

If the banned number is the one printed on your visiting cards, bills and shop board, you lose years of customer chats in a day. We do not offer bulk-sender campaigns at Inovix, at any price.

2. What WhatsApp marketing services in Panipat include

Opt-in list building

Everything starts with permission. Practical ways to collect it:

  • A QR code at the billing counter: "Scan to get new stock and offers on WhatsApp".
  • A tick-box on your website enquiry form.
  • A WhatsApp button on your site and Google listing; a customer who messages you first has started the conversation.
  • A line on the invoice or packing slip inviting customers to join.

Broadcast campaigns through the official API

Once you have a list, campaigns are sent as approved template messages: a new collection launch for a home-furnishing showroom, a winter blanket rate list for wholesale buyers, a health check-up camp for a clinic, admission dates for a coaching centre. Lists are segmented so that a retailer in Karnal and a walk-in customer from Model Town do not receive the same message.

Catalog

Your products with photos, prices and descriptions sit inside WhatsApp itself. A buyer can browse, pick items and send a cart without leaving the chat. For traders with a steady range, this alone reduces the "photo bhejo, rate bhejo" back-and-forth.

Click-to-WhatsApp ads

These are Facebook and Instagram ads where the button opens a chat with you instead of a website. They suit businesses whose customers prefer to ask before buying, which is most local businesses. The person messages you first, so the chat opens a free reply window and they become a contact you may follow up with, with their consent.

Automated journeys

Welcome message, catalogue on request, reminder if the cart is not confirmed, thank-you and review request after purchase. The technical side is covered in our guide to WhatsApp Business API automation.

3. The rules that keep your number safe

  • Opt-in is compulsory. Message only contacts who agreed, and keep a record of how they agreed.
  • Use approved templates for any message you start. Promotional content must be submitted in the marketing category.
  • Offer an easy exit. "Reply STOP to unsubscribe", and actually remove them.
  • Do not over-send. For most local businesses, two to four useful messages a month is plenty. When people block or report you, Meta lowers your quality rating and sending limit.
  • Never buy databases. Those people do not know you, and they will report you.

4. How Inovix sets up a campaign

  1. Check the basics. Is your WhatsApp Business profile complete? Often the free app with labels, catalog and small broadcast lists is enough for now, and we will say so.
  2. API setup in your name. Meta business verification, number registration and display name. The number and the Meta account belong to you, not to us.
  3. List and segments. Import existing opted-in customers and set up the QR and form opt-ins.
  4. Write templates. Short, clear, in the language your customers use, with one action button.
  5. Send, measure, adjust. We look at read and reply rates and stop sending what people ignore.
  6. Connect replies to a person. A campaign that generates 80 replies with nobody answering is wasted money.

5. What it costs

There is no published Inovix price for WhatsApp marketing; you get a scope-based quote on WhatsApp before work starts. The outside costs have three parts:

  • Meta's charges. Marketing template messages are charged per message, roughly under a rupee each in India at the time of writing. Rates and rules are revised from time to time, so check the current official WhatsApp Business Platform pricing.
  • Platform fee. API providers usually charge a monthly subscription, commonly from around ₹1,000 to a few thousand rupees for small plans, as a market estimate.
  • Ad spend for click-to-WhatsApp ads, paid directly to Meta from your own ad account.

If you want campaigns linked to your billing or CRM, that is custom work; our Custom Software & CRM projects with WhatsApp Business API bots start at ₹49,999+ (see pricing).

6. What results to expect

We do not promise a number of leads; nobody honestly can. What is generally true is that messages to a clean opt-in list get read far more than email or SMS, and that results depend on the offer and on how quickly your team replies. Start small, measure one campaign, then scale. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is bulk WhatsApp marketing legal and safe?
Sending promotions to purchased or scraped numbers with unofficial bulk-sender software breaks WhatsApp's terms, and numbers used this way are regularly banned. The safe route is the official WhatsApp Business API with approved templates, sent only to contacts who opted in.
Q: How much do WhatsApp marketing services cost in Panipat?
Inovix shares a scope-based quote on WhatsApp before work starts. Separately, Meta charges per marketing message (roughly under a rupee each in India, subject to change) and API platforms charge a monthly fee. Always check current official pricing.
Q: How do I get customers to opt in?
Use a QR code at your counter, a tick-box on website forms, a WhatsApp button on your site and Google listing, and a line on invoices. Customers who message you first have started the conversation, and you can ask them there if they want updates.
Q: Do I need the API or is the WhatsApp Business app enough?
For a small list, the free WhatsApp Business app with catalog, labels and broadcast lists to people who saved your number can be enough. The API is worth it when you need larger campaigns, tracking, several staff on one number or automation.
