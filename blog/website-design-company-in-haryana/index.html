Website Design Company in Haryana: District-Wise Guide, Prices & What to Check (2026)

A factory in Faridabad and a coaching centre in Rohtak do not need the same website. Here is how to choose by region, industry and budget, with a page for every district.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Search for a website design company in Haryana and you get hundreds of names, from one-person freelancers to big Gurugram agencies. The problem is not finding a company. The problem is knowing what your business actually needs, because Haryana is not one market. An exporter in Panipat, a clinic in Karnal and a SaaS startup in Gurugram need three different websites. This guide breaks the state down by region and industry, shows our fixed prices, and links to a detailed page for each of the 22 districts.

1. Haryana is four markets, not one

NCR belt: Gurugram, Faridabad, Sonipat, Jhajjar, Rewari, Palwal

Gurugram is a corporate and IT city, so buyers expect a polished, fast, professional site. Faridabad, Bahadurgarh, Kundli and Bawal are industrial areas where most business is B2B. Here the website is a digital catalogue: product pages, specifications, certifications and an enquiry form that a purchase manager can fill in two minutes.

GT Road belt: Panipat, Karnal, Kurukshetra, Ambala, Yamunanagar

Panipat sells textiles and handloom across India and abroad. Karnal is known for rice and agri businesses, Ambala for scientific instruments, Yamunanagar for plywood. Many buyers sit in other states or other countries, so the site must look trustworthy to a stranger: clear product photos, company details, GST and export information, and a WhatsApp button for quick quotes.

Central and western Haryana: Rohtak, Hisar, Jind, Bhiwani, Sirsa, Fatehabad, Kaithal

Rohtak and Hisar are strong education and medical towns, and Hisar also has a steel industry. Coaching institutes, schools, hospitals, labs and showrooms dominate. Customers search on mobile, often in Hindi or Hinglish, and call within minutes. A simple, fast site with fees or service lists, location and a call button beats a fancy design.

Northern edge: Panchkula

Panchkula behaves like part of the Chandigarh Tricity. Competition on Google is higher, so service pages and a well-kept Google Business Profile matter more.

3. What a web development company in Haryana should give you

Whoever you hire, get these five points in writing before paying an advance:

  1. Domain and hosting in your name, with logins shared with you.
  2. Mobile speed — ask to open one of their live sites on your own phone.
  3. Source code ownership, so you can change developer any time.
  4. Local SEO basics — Google Business Profile, Maps, correct name, address and phone.
  5. Stage-wise payment and a clear monthly cost after launch.

Be careful with anyone who guarantees the first position on Google. Nobody controls Google. An honest company promises the work, not the rank.
