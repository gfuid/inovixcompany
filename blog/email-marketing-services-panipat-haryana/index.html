Email Marketing Services in Panipat & Haryana: A Practical Guide for Exporters and B2B

Overseas buyers do not place orders on WhatsApp forwards. They read email, and half the battle is making sure yours reaches the inbox.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

A home-furnishing exporter returns from a trade fair with 150 visiting cards. Two weeks later, ten buyers have received a proper follow-up and the rest are still in a drawer. Meanwhile the catalogue PDF sent from a free Gmail ID has landed in spam. This is the everyday reality that email marketing services in Panipat & Haryana should solve. For local retail, WhatsApp is king. But for exporters and B2B suppliers dealing with importers, buying houses and corporate purchase teams, email is still where business is done.

1. Who really needs email marketing services in Panipat & Haryana

  • Textile, handloom and home-furnishing exporters who meet buyers at fairs and through B2B portals and need to stay in touch across long buying cycles.
  • Manufacturers and industrial suppliers across Haryana selling to purchase departments.
  • Schools, colleges and coaching institutes sending circulars and admission updates to parents who gave their email.
  • Online stores for order emails, abandoned-cart reminders and repeat-purchase offers.

If your customers are mainly walk-in locals, be honest with yourself: put the budget into your Google listing and WhatsApp first.

2. The three kinds of email that bring business

Buyer follow-up sequences

A short, planned series after first contact: a thank-you with your catalogue link on day one, product details matched to what the buyer asked about a few days later, a sampling offer after that, then a gentle check-in. Each email is short, personal and sent from a real person's name. The sequence stops automatically the moment the buyer replies.

Newsletters

Once or twice a month: new collections, new certifications, capacity updates, fair schedules. Buyers may not order today, but when their current supplier slips, they remember the one who stayed visible.

Transactional and reminder emails

Proforma invoices, dispatch updates, payment reminders and document sharing, sent automatically from your CRM or billing system. This overlaps with custom CRM software, where the email step is built into the order flow.

3. Deliverability: SPF, DKIM and DMARC in simple words

Deliverability means your email reaches the inbox instead of spam. Major mailbox providers such as Gmail and Yahoo now expect senders, especially bulk senders, to authenticate their mail. Three DNS records do this:

RecordWhat it doesSimple comparison
SPFLists which servers are allowed to send email for your domainThe authorised signatory list at your bank
DKIMAdds a digital signature proving the email was not alteredThe company seal on a letter
DMARCTells receiving servers what to do if SPF or DKIM fail, and sends you reportsThe instruction "reject any cheque without seal and signature"

Beyond these records, a few habits matter just as much:

  • Send from your own domain (name@yourcompany.com), not a free Gmail or Yahoo address. It looks professional and is required for authentication.
  • Warm up slowly. A new domain that suddenly sends thousands of emails looks like a spammer. Start with small batches.
  • Keep marketing mail separate. Many businesses use a subdomain for newsletters so a campaign problem does not affect daily business email.
  • Write like a person. Heavy images, ALL CAPS and "100% FREE!!!" subject lines trigger filters.

4. List hygiene: the rule that protects everything else

Email works only with permission-based lists. That means people who gave you their card, enquired, bought from you or signed up. It does not mean a purchased "50,000 importers database".

  • No purchased or scraped lists. They are full of dead and trap addresses, damage your domain reputation and can break the anti-spam and privacy laws of your buyers' countries.
  • Unsubscribe link in every marketing email, honoured immediately.
  • Remove bounces. Addresses that hard-bounce should be deleted after the first failure.
  • Trim the inactive. Contacts who have not opened anything in many months should get one re-confirmation email and then be removed.
  • Segment. A US home-textile importer and a domestic distributor should not receive the same email.
  • Record the source of every contact: which fair, which form, which year. If you email buyers in Europe or elsewhere, their local rules on consent apply too, so check them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why do my business emails go to spam?
Common reasons are missing SPF, DKIM or DMARC records, sending from a free email address, a new domain sending too many emails too fast, purchased lists with dead addresses and spammy subject lines. Fixing authentication and cleaning the list solves most cases.
Q: Can I buy an importers email database and send my catalogue?
You should not. Purchased lists contain invalid and trap addresses that damage your domain reputation, and unsolicited bulk email can break anti-spam and privacy laws in buyers' countries. Use permission-based contacts from fairs, enquiries and customers.
Q: How much do email marketing services cost in Panipat and Haryana?
Inovix gives a scope-based quote on WhatsApp before work starts. As a market estimate, email tools range from free tiers to a few thousand rupees a month, and management by an agency is typically quoted from the low thousands to around Rs 15,000 a month depending on scope.
Q: Is email marketing better than WhatsApp marketing?
They suit different audiences. Overseas buyers, purchase departments and corporate clients prefer email. Local retail customers respond better on WhatsApp. Many exporters use email for buyers and WhatsApp for domestic dealers, both with permission.
