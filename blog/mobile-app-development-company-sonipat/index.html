Mobile App Development Company in Sonipat: Android, iOS & PWA Cost Guide 2026

Before you spend lakhs on an app, understand the three options, the real costs and whether your Sonipat business needs an app at all.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Many business owners contact a mobile app development company in Sonipat with one line: "Mujhe ek app banwana hai." The honest first question should be: what should the app do, and who will open it every day? An app that nobody uses is just an expensive icon. This guide helps Sonipat, Kundli and Rai businesses choose between Android, iOS and PWA, understand typical 2026 costs, and avoid paying for something they do not need.

1. Who in Sonipat actually needs a mobile app

Sonipat has a mix that is quite different from other Haryana cities. On one side are the industrial belts of Kundli, Rai and Barhi with manufacturers and B2B suppliers. On the other side is the education hub — Rajiv Gandhi Education City at Rai and several universities in and around the district — plus schools, coaching centres, hostels and PGs. Along the highway, Murthal's food businesses see heavy daily footfall.

Apps make sense where the same people come back again and again:

  • Manufacturers and distributors: dealer ordering apps, sales-staff apps for field visits, dispatch tracking.
  • Schools and coaching institutes: attendance, fees, homework, test results and notices for parents and students.
  • Hostels and PGs: rent reminders, complaints, visitor entry.
  • Restaurants and cloud kitchens: repeat ordering and loyalty points.
  • Clinics and labs: appointments and report downloads.
  • Local service startups: booking and tracking.

If your customers buy from you once a year, you probably need a good website, not an app. Our website vs web app vs mobile app guide helps you decide.

2. Android, iOS or PWA: what a mobile app development company in Sonipat should explain

There are three main routes. A good developer will explain all three instead of pushing the costliest one.

OptionWhat it isBest forTypical 2026 cost (estimate)
PWA (Progressive Web App)A web app that installs from the browser, works on Android, iPhone and desktopDealer portals, staff apps, school apps, internal tools₹50,000 – ₹2 lakh
Android appApp published on Google PlayCustomer apps where most users are on Android₹80,000 – ₹4 lakh
Android + iOS (cross-platform)One codebase published on both storesConsumer apps, startups, premium audiences₹1.5 lakh – ₹8 lakh+

These are typical market ranges, not fixed rates. Price moves with the number of screens, logins, payment gateway, notifications and admin panel.

Why we often suggest a PWA first

For most small and mid-size businesses, a PWA is the smartest starting point. There is one build for all devices, no app-store approval wait, updates go live instantly, and the cost is much lower. Staff and dealers add it to their home screen and use it like a normal app. If the idea works and you later need Play Store presence, the same backend can power a store app.

3. Costs people forget to ask about

The build quote is only one part. Ask about these before signing:

  • Store accounts: Google Play has a one-time developer registration fee and Apple has a yearly developer fee, charged by Google and Apple directly.
  • Server and database: a monthly hosting cost that grows with users.
  • SMS, OTP and WhatsApp messages: charged per message by the provider.
  • Payment gateway charges: a percentage on each transaction.
  • Maintenance: Android and iOS release new versions every year, and apps need updates to keep working.

An agency that hides these is not giving you the full picture.

6. Mistakes to avoid

  • Building everything in version one. Start with one core job and add features after real users give feedback.
  • Copying a big brand's app. Those apps cost crores and have full teams behind them. Your app needs to solve your customers' one problem well.
  • No admin panel. Without it you will call the developer for every small change.
  • Not owning the code or store account. Publish under your own company's developer account and insist on source-code handoff.
  • Ignoring the website. People still search on Google first. If you also need a site, see our guide for website design and development in Sonipat.

A Progressive Web App is a web application that installs from the browser and works on Android, iPhone and desktop. For dealer portals, staff apps and school apps it is usually enough and costs much less than store apps.
