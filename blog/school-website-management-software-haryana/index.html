School Website Design and School Management Software in Haryana: 2026 Guide

A practical guide for school owners and principals: what to build first, what it costs, and how to avoid paying yearly for software your staff never uses.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Most parents now check a school on Google before they visit the campus. If your school has no website, or an old one that does not open properly on a phone, you lose admission enquiries to the school down the road. This guide explains school website design and school management software for schools in Haryana in simple terms: what each part does, what it typically costs in 2026, and in which order you should build it.

1. Why Haryana schools need more than a brochure website

A brochure website shows photos and a phone number. That was fine ten years ago. Today a parent in Panipat, Karnal, Sonipat or Rohtak expects to see the fee structure range, transport routes, board affiliation, facilities and an easy way to ask a question on WhatsApp. At the same time, your office staff is busy with fee registers, attendance sheets and result preparation in Excel.

So a school really has two separate needs:

  • A public website that brings admission enquiries.
  • Internal software that reduces office work: fee, attendance, results and parent communication.

You do not have to buy both on day one. Many schools start with the website before the admission season and add software modules later.

3. What a good school admission website must have

The website has one goal: turn a curious parent into an enquiry. Keep it fast and clear.

  • Mobile-first design. Most parents will open it on a phone, often on mobile data.
  • Admission enquiry form with only 4–5 fields: parent name, phone, class, village or city, message.
  • 1-click WhatsApp button so a parent can chat with the admission desk directly.
  • Clear pages for academics, facilities, transport, gallery, mandatory disclosure (for CBSE schools) and contact.
  • Google Maps and Google Business Profile set up correctly, so the school appears for searches like "best school near me".
  • Hindi or bilingual content where your parents prefer it.

Schools with multiple branches or many pages usually choose the Growth Engine plan at ₹19,999, which is a multi-page React/Vite site with targeted SEO and lead capture alerts.

4. Software modules: fee, attendance and results

Fee management

This is the module that pays for itself. You define fee heads (tuition, transport, exam), the system generates receipts and shows a live list of pending dues class-wise. A polite reminder can go to parents before the due date instead of an awkward phone call after it.

Attendance

The class teacher marks attendance on a phone in under a minute. Absent students' parents can get a message the same morning. Monthly attendance percentages are ready for report cards without manual counting.

Results and report cards

Teachers enter marks subject-wise, the system calculates totals and grades in your format and produces report card PDFs. Parents can view the result with a login or a secure link.

Role-based access

The accountant should see fees, not marks. A teacher should see only her classes. The principal sees everything. Good custom software is built with these roles from the start.

5. Parent WhatsApp updates: the feature parents actually notice

Parents rarely install one more app, but almost every parent in Haryana uses WhatsApp. Fee reminders, absent alerts, holiday notices and PTM invitations sent on WhatsApp get read. For a few messages you can use the normal WhatsApp Business app. For automatic messages to hundreds of parents you need the official WhatsApp Business API, where Meta charges a small per-message fee for template messages. This fee changes from time to time, so always check the current official rate before budgeting.

Important: take written consent from parents on the admission form for WhatsApp communication, and keep student data access limited to staff who need it.

6. Ready-made school ERP vs custom software

Ready-made school ERPs usually charge per student per year. They work well if your process matches theirs. Problems start when your fee structure, report card format or transport billing is different, or when the yearly bill grows with strength.

Custom software costs more at the start but is built around your process, and you own it. At Inovix, custom software and CRM projects start at ₹49,999+, are built in 14–28 day sprints, and come with full source-code handoff. There is no per-student licence fee from us; you only pay ongoing hosting and maintenance. You can read a detailed breakdown in our guide on custom software development cost in India.

A simple rule: if you are under roughly 300 students and happy with standard formats, try a ready-made tool first. If you run a larger school or multiple branches and keep fighting your current software, custom is usually cheaper over three to four years.

7. Suggested rollout plan for a school

  1. Before admission season: launch the admission website and Google Business Profile.
  2. Next: fee module with receipts and pending dues, because it gives the quickest return.
  3. Then: attendance and parent WhatsApp alerts.
  4. Finally: results, report cards and any transport or staff modules.

We work on milestone payments (30% / 40% / 30%), so you pay as you see working software, not everything in advance. See how we run projects on our process page.

8. Questions to ask any vendor before you sign

  • Who owns the source code and the student data?
  • Can I export all data to Excel any time?
  • Is there a per-student or per-year licence fee?
  • Who trains my staff, and who do I call when something breaks?
  • Does it work well on a basic Android phone?
