Mobile App Development Company in Haryana: Android, iOS or PWA — What Should You Build?

Not every business needs a Play Store app. Start with who will use it and how often, then pick the cheapest route that does the job.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

"Ek app banwani hai" is one of the most common messages we receive. Before choosing a mobile app development company in Haryana, it is worth spending ten minutes on a more basic question: what kind of app, for whom, and is an app even the right tool? The wrong choice here can double your cost. This guide covers the Android, iOS and PWA decision, where apps genuinely help Haryana businesses, how the build happens and what store publishing involves.

1. First decision: who will open this app every week?

An app only works if people keep it on their phone. That happens when they use it often. So sort your idea into one of three groups:

  • Your own staff or dealers use it daily (orders, attendance, field visits). Strong case for an app.
  • Repeat customers use it weekly (students, patients on treatment, regular food orders, society residents). Good case.
  • One-time customers who buy once a year. Weak case. A fast website with WhatsApp will bring more business for far less money.

If you are not sure which you need, our guide on website vs web app vs mobile app explains the difference with examples.

2. Android, iOS or PWA: an honest comparison

OptionWhat it isBest whenLimits
PWA (progressive web app)A web app that installs from the browser and opens like an appInternal tools, dealer ordering, booking, tight budget, fast launchNot listed in stores by default; some device features are restricted, especially on iPhone
Android appApp published on Google PlayMost Haryana customers and staff, who are largely on AndroidPlay Store review and policies to follow
Android + iOS (React Native)One codebase, two store appsConsumer apps in Gurugram, Panchkula, urban premium usersHigher cost and two store accounts to maintain

A practical rule for most small and mid-size businesses in the state: start with a PWA or Android, prove people use it, then add iOS. Building everything on day one is how budgets get burned.

3. Where apps earn their cost in Haryana

Factories and distributors

Units in Faridabad, Bawal, Kundli and Panipat often run sales through dealers and field staff. A dealer ordering app with price lists, order status and outstanding balance removes endless phone calls and WhatsApp photos of handwritten orders.

Schools, colleges and coaching

Rohtak, Hisar, Sonipat and Kurukshetra have large education markets. Parents and students actually open an app that shows attendance, fees due, homework, test results and notices.

Clinics, hospitals and labs

Appointment booking, report download and follow-up reminders. Health data is sensitive, so access control and secure storage must be part of the plan from the start.

Agri and trading businesses

Around Karnal, Kaithal and Sirsa, traders and input dealers can use simple apps for rates, orders and khata. Here Hindi language support and a very simple screen matter more than design.

Local delivery and services

Restaurants, grocery, laundry and home services with repeat orders. Often a PWA linked to WhatsApp is enough at the beginning.

4. How an app is built, step by step

  1. Scope call: we list users, screens and the three or four actions that matter most. Everything else waits for version two.
  2. Clickable design: you see and tap through screens before code is written. Changes are cheap at this stage.
  3. Build in sprints: you get a working test build every week or two, not a surprise at the end.
  4. Admin panel: a web dashboard for you to manage users, orders and content.
  5. Testing on real phones, including low-cost Android devices and slow networks, because that is what many users have.
  6. Launch and handover: store publishing, source code and all accounts handed to you.

At Inovix we use React Native for store apps and React-based PWAs, with Node or Python and Supabase on the backend. You can see the working style on our process page.

5. Store publishing: what owners should know

  • Developer accounts should be in your company's name. Google Play charges a one-time registration fee and Apple charges a yearly fee. Amounts change, so check the current official pricing.
  • Verification takes time. Both stores verify identity or business details, and new Google Play accounts may have testing requirements before public release. Start this early.
  • You need a privacy policy and honest declarations about what data the app collects.
  • Review is not instant. Apps can be rejected and need changes. Keep a buffer before any launch date you announce.
  • Apps need upkeep. Android and iOS update every year, and stores expect apps to keep up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much does it cost to build a mobile app in Haryana?
It depends on screens, user types, backend and platforms. Inovix has no fixed published app price; a scope-based quote is shared on WhatsApp before work starts. Custom software with an admin panel starts at ₹49,999 on our pricing page.
Q: Should I build an Android app, iOS app or PWA first?
For most Haryana businesses a PWA or Android app is the sensible first step, since most users are on Android. Add iOS once usage is proven or if your customers are mainly iPhone users.
Q: Who owns the app and the Play Store account?
You do. Developer accounts are created in your company's name, and Inovix hands over 100% of the source code and all server and store access.
Q: How long does app development take?
A simple PWA or small app can be ready in a few weeks, while multi-role apps with payments and admin panels take longer. Store verification and review add extra time, so plan a buffer before launch.
