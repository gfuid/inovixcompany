Ecommerce Website Development Cost in India 2026: Full Breakdown

The store build is only one line of the budget. Here is the full picture, from setup to the charges you pay on every single order.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Most people who ask about ecommerce website development cost in India get one number: the build price. But an online store has three layers of cost. There is the build, the per-order charges (payment gateway and shipping), and the monthly running cost. If you budget only for the first, the other two will eat your margin. This guide covers all three in simple terms.

The ranges below are typical 2026 market estimates, not official figures. Platform and gateway prices change often, so confirm the current rate on the official website before you decide.

1. Ecommerce Website Development Cost in India by Platform

There are three common ways to build a store. Each has a different cost shape.

Notice the trade-off. Shopify is cheap to start and more expensive to run. Custom is expensive to start and cheap to run. WooCommerce sits in the middle but needs regular technical care. We compare the first and third options in depth in Shopify vs custom ecommerce website.

2. What Changes the Build Price

  • Number of products and variants. Uploading 30 products is a day of work. Uploading 3,000 with sizes, colours and separate photos is a project in itself.
  • Design. A standard theme with your branding is the cheapest. Custom product pages, lookbooks and animations add cost.
  • Pricing logic. Wholesale slabs, dealer logins, minimum order quantities, GST invoices with HSN codes, COD rules by pincode. Standard themes do not handle these well.
  • Integrations. Shipping aggregator, accounting software, WhatsApp order updates, marketplace sync.
  • Content. Product photography and descriptions are often the most underestimated cost. Bad photos sell nothing on any platform.

3. Payment Gateway Charges

To accept UPI, cards and netbanking you need a payment gateway such as Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree or PhonePe. Most Indian gateways charge no setup fee and take a percentage of every transaction, typically around 2% plus GST for common payment methods. Rates can differ for international cards, EMI and certain wallets, and they change from time to time, so check the gateway's current pricing page.

Points to plan for:

  • KYC takes time. You will need a bank account, PAN, business proof and a website with policy pages (refund, shipping, terms, privacy). Approval can take from a couple of days to a couple of weeks.
  • Settlement cycle. Money usually reaches your bank a few working days after the order, not instantly.
  • Shopify extra fee. On Shopify, using a third-party gateway usually attracts an additional Shopify transaction fee on top of the gateway's own charge. Confirm this for your plan.

4. Shipping and Logistics Integration

Most small stores in India ship through aggregators like Shiprocket, Delhivery or similar services. They give you one dashboard for multiple couriers, label printing and tracking.

  • Per-shipment cost: for a 500 g prepaid parcel within India, rates typically start somewhere in the ₹30 – ₹80 range depending on zone and volume. Heavier and bulky items cost much more because of volumetric weight.
  • COD charges: cash on delivery carries an extra fee per order, and COD orders have a much higher return rate than prepaid ones.
  • RTO (return to origin): when a customer refuses the parcel, you pay shipping both ways. For fashion and low-ticket COD products this is often the single biggest hidden cost.
  • Integration work: connecting the aggregator to Shopify or WooCommerce is usually a plugin. For a custom store it is API development, typically a few days of work.

5. Monthly Running Costs to Budget

Here is a rough monthly picture for a small store doing around 200 orders with an average order value of ₹1,000. Treat it as an illustration, not a promise.

  • Payment gateway at about 2% on prepaid orders: roughly ₹2,000 – ₹4,000
  • Shipping: roughly ₹10,000 – ₹18,000 (often partly recovered from the customer)
  • Platform or hosting: from a few hundred rupees (custom or WooCommerce) to several thousand (Shopify with apps)
  • Domain and business email: small, but yearly
  • Maintenance: see our website AMC guide
  • Marketing: usually the biggest line. A store with no ads, no SEO and no Instagram gets no visitors.

6. Which Option Fits Your Budget?

  • Testing an idea with under 50 products: start with a simple catalogue site and WhatsApp ordering, or a basic Shopify plan. Do not spend lakhs before you have your first 100 orders.
  • Growing D2C brand: Shopify is a solid choice. The app ecosystem saves development time and you can launch in days.
  • Wholesale, B2B, manufacturers, textile and handloom exporters: custom usually wins because dealer pricing, bulk enquiry flows and GST invoicing rarely fit a standard theme.
  • Already on WordPress with a strong blog: WooCommerce keeps everything in one place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the minimum cost to start an ecommerce website in India?
You can start with a catalogue website and WhatsApp ordering for roughly ₹5,000 to ₹25,000, or a theme-based Shopify store for roughly ₹10,000 to ₹60,000 in setup plus the monthly plan fee. A full custom store typically starts around ₹60,000 and goes up with features.
Q: How much do payment gateways charge in India?
Most Indian gateways charge no setup fee and take around 2% plus GST per transaction for common methods like UPI via gateway, cards and netbanking. Rates vary by payment method and change over time, so check the official pricing page of the gateway you choose.
Q: Is Shopify cheaper than a custom ecommerce website?
Shopify is cheaper to start but has a monthly plan fee, paid apps and possible extra transaction fees. A custom store costs more upfront but has low running costs. Over two to three years, a custom store can be cheaper for businesses with steady sales.
Q: How long does it take to build an ecommerce website?
A theme-based Shopify store can go live in one to two weeks if products and photos are ready. WooCommerce stores usually take three to six weeks. Custom stores typically take four to ten weeks depending on features. Payment gateway KYC can add a few days.
