Website Se Leads Kaise Laye: Lead Generation Website Ka Simple 2026 Guide

Website sirf visiting card nahi hai. Sahi tarike se bani ho to yeh roz enquiry laane wala salesman ban sakti hai. Yahan step-by-step samjhiye.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Bahut se business owners ki ek hi shikayat hoti hai: "Website to banwa li, par ek bhi call nahi aaya." Problem website hone mein nahi, website ke kaam karne ke tarike mein hoti hai. Agar aap soch rahe hain ki website se leads kaise laye, to yeh guide aapke liye hai. Hum simple Hinglish mein woh 7 cheezein samjhayenge jo ek sundar dikhne wali website aur ek lead generation website ke beech ka fark banati hain.

1. Website se leads kaise laye: pehle basic samjhiye

Lead ka matlab hai ek aisa insaan jisne aapke product ya service mein interest dikhaya aur apna contact diya, ya aapko message ya call kiya. Website se lead aane ke liye teen cheezein chahiye:

  1. Sahi log website par aayein (traffic) — Google search, Google Maps, ads ya social media se.
  2. Unhe bharosa ho ki aap genuine ho aur unka kaam kar sakte ho.
  3. Contact karna bahut aasaan ho — ek click mein.

In teeno mein se ek bhi kamzor hui to lead nahi aayegi. Ab ek-ek karke dekhte hain.

2. Website ki speed: 3 second ka rule

Aapka customer mobile par hai, aksar slow network par. Agar website khulne mein zyada time leti hai to woh back button daba kar competitor ke paas chala jata hai. Heavy images, purane templates aur sasti hosting speed ke sabse bade dushman hain.

  • Photos compress karke lagayein.
  • Faltu sliders, popups aur animations hatayein.
  • Achhi hosting aur modern technology use karein. Hum React, Vite aur Cloudflare/Vercel par websites banate hain taaki mobile par jaldi khulein.
  • Apni site ko khud mobile data par khol kar check karein, sirf office ke Wi-Fi par nahi.

Aap hamare free tools page par bhi kuch kaam ki cheezein dekh sakte hain.

3. WhatsApp button aur clear call-to-action

India mein log form bharne se zyada WhatsApp par message karna pasand karte hain. Isliye har page par ek 1-click WhatsApp button hona chahiye, jisme message pehle se likha ho, jaise "Hi, mujhe aapki service ke baare mein jaankari chahiye".

  • Mobile par neeche hamesha dikhne wala Call aur WhatsApp button.
  • Har section ke baad ek clear action: "Free quote lein", "Aaj hi appointment book karein".
  • Form mein sirf 3 field: naam, phone, zarurat. Jitne zyada field, utni kam leads.
  • "Contact us" jaise general button ki jagah fayda likhein: "2 minute mein rate jaanein".

4. Google Business Profile: local customer yahin se aata hai

Jab koi "near me" search karta hai, Google sabse pehle map wali listing dikhata hai. Yeh free hai aur local business ke liye sabse zyada kaam ki cheez hai.

  • Naam, address, phone aur timing bilkul sahi rakhein.
  • Sahi category chunein aur services add karein.
  • Asli photos daalte rahein.
  • Khush customers se review maangein aur har review ka jawab dein.
  • Profile mein apni website aur WhatsApp link zaroor lagayein.

Hamare Launchpad Website plan (₹4,999) mein Google Maps aur local SEO setup included hai. Detail mein padhne ke liye yeh guide dekhein: Google Business Profile SEO.

5. Landing pages: ek service, ek page

Agar aap AC repair, RO service aur fridge repair teeno karte hain, to teeno ke liye alag page banayein. Jo insaan "AC repair Panipat" search karke aaya hai, use seedha AC repair ka page dikhna chahiye, home page nahi.

Ek achhe landing page mein yeh hota hai:

  • Upar clear headline: aap kya karte ho aur kahan.
  • 3–4 fayde, short mein.
  • Asli kaam ki photos. Jhoothe review ya nakli numbers mat lagayein; customer pakad leta hai.
  • Price ya starting price, agar bata sakte hain.
  • Common sawaalon ke jawab (FAQ).
  • Upar aur neeche dono jagah WhatsApp aur Call button.

Google Ads chalate hain to landing page aur bhi zaroori hai, warna paisa click par kharch hoga aur lead nahi banegi. Ads aur SEO ka fark samajhne ke liye padhein: Google Ads vs SEO.

6. Bharosa banane wali cheezein

Online customer aapko jaanta nahi. Use yakeen dilana padta hai:

  • Dukaan, team aur kaam ki asli photos.
  • Poora address, map aur GST number (agar hai).
  • Google reviews ka link.
  • Saaf pricing ya process: kaam kaise hoga, kitne din lagenge.
  • Owner ka naam aur direct number. Log insaan se baat karna chahte hain, company se nahi.

7. Follow-up: asli paisa yahin banta hai

Lead aana aadha kaam hai. Zyada tar businesses yahin haar jate hain kyunki enquiry ka jawab der se dete hain ya dobara call hi nahi karte.

KabKya karein
5–10 minute ke andarCall ya WhatsApp par pehla jawab
Usi dinRate, details ya brochure bhejein
2–3 din baadHalka sa reminder: "Koi sawaal ho to bataiye"
1 hafte baadAakhri follow-up, koi offer ho to batayein
Har leadSheet ya CRM mein note karein: kahan se aayi, kya hua

Shuru mein ek simple Google Sheet kaafi hai. Jab leads badh jayein to chhota CRM banwa sakte hain jo har nayi enquiry par aapko WhatsApp alert bheje aur follow-up yaad dilaye. Hamare Growth Engine plan (₹19,999) mein lead capture aur alerts included hain.

8. Common galtiyan jo leads rok deti hain

  • Website par phone number image mein likha hai, click nahi hota.
  • Form bharne ke baad enquiry kisi ko milti hi nahi. Khud test karke dekhein.
  • Sirf home page hai, service-wise page nahi.
  • Website banwa kar bhool gaye; na Google profile, na koi promotion.
  • Sabse sasti website le li jo slow hai aur Google par dikhti nahi. Iske baare mein padhein: ₹500 website ka sach.

9. Ab aage kya karein

Ek baat seedhi hai: koi bhi agency leads ki guarantee nahi de sakti, aur jo de rahi hai us se savdhan rahein. Lekin upar ki 7 cheezein sahi ho to enquiry aane ke chances kaafi badh jate hain. Agar aap chahte hain ki hum aapki current website dekh kar batayein ki leads kahan ruk rahi hain, to WhatsApp par link bhej dijiye. Plans aur rates pricing page par clear likhe hain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Website banane ke kitne din baad leads aani shuru hoti hain?
Yeh traffic par depend karta hai. Google Ads ya apne WhatsApp contacts mein share karne se kuch dinon mein enquiry aa sakti hai. SEO aur Google Business Profile se results aane mein aam taur par kuch mahine lagte hain. Koi fixed guarantee nahi hoti.
Q: Kya sirf WhatsApp button lagane se leads badh jati hain?
Akela button kaafi nahi hai, lekin yeh contact karna bahut aasaan bana deta hai. Fast website, clear offer aur Google Business Profile ke saath milkar yeh achha kaam karta hai.
Q: Lead generation website banwane ka kharcha kitna hai?
Inovix mein Launchpad Website ₹4,999 one-time hai jisme WhatsApp lead button aur Google Maps setup milta hai. Multi-page Growth Engine ₹19,999 hai jisme targeted SEO aur lead capture alerts hain. Hosting aur care ₹500 per month hai.
Q: Kya mujhe leads ke liye Google Ads chalane hi padenge?
Zaroori nahi. Local business ke liye Google Business Profile aur achhe service pages se bhi enquiry aati hai. Jaldi results chahiye to chhote budget se ads test kar sakte hain.
