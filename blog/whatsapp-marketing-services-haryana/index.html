WhatsApp Marketing Services in Haryana for Multi-Branch Businesses and Dealer Networks

One phone with the WhatsApp Business app works for one shop. Five branches or two hundred dealers need a different setup, and stricter rules.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

For a single shop, WhatsApp marketing is easy: one phone, one broadcast list. It gets complicated when you have branches in Panipat, Karnal and Rohtak, or two hundred dealers from Ambala to Rewari. Different staff use personal numbers, nobody knows who received which offer, and one day a number gets banned. This guide to WhatsApp marketing services in Haryana is written for exactly these multi-city businesses: what the official setup looks like, how to collect opt-ins, how costs work and how to connect it to a chatbot and CRM.

If you have one location and a few hundred customers, stay on the free app. Once you have multiple branches, a sales team or a dealer network, the API is the right tool. Our general guide to WhatsApp Business API automation covers the basics; here we focus on the multi-city setup.

2. The rule that matters most: opt-in and the official API

Many sellers in the market offer "bulk WhatsApp" software that sends lakhs of messages to purchased numbers from ordinary SIMs. Do not use it. It breaks WhatsApp's terms, recipients report the messages as spam, and numbers get banned, sometimes including your main business number. It also damages your name with the very customers you want.

The correct way has two parts: send only through the official WhatsApp Business API, and send only to people who agreed to receive your messages. Ways to collect opt-in across branches:

  • A tick box on bills, admission forms, dealer registration forms and your website form.
  • A QR code at each branch counter: "Scan to get offers and updates on WhatsApp".
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ads, where the customer messages you first.
  • A missed call or keyword, such as sending "JOIN" to your number.

Record when and where each person opted in, and honour every "STOP" request immediately.

3. Campaign types that work for branches and dealers

Dealer and distributor communication

For manufacturers in Yamunanagar plywood, Panipat textiles, Hisar steel products or agri inputs around Karnal and Sirsa: new price lists, scheme announcements, stock availability, dispatch updates with LR details and outstanding payment reminders. Each dealer receives only their own figures, pulled from your system.

Branch-wise customer offers

A diagnostic lab, jewellery chain, coaching group or showroom with several outlets can send an offer only to customers of the relevant branch, with that branch's address and phone. Replies go to that branch's staff, not to head office.

Transactional updates

Order confirmation, delivery, fee receipt, appointment reminder, report ready. These are the most welcome messages and build the habit of customers reading you.

Re-activation

A polite message to opted-in customers who have not bought for a few months. Keep it rare. Too many promotional messages lead to blocks, and blocks reduce your number's quality rating.

4. Hindi templates: write the way your customers read

Every business-initiated message on the API must use a template approved by Meta. Templates can be created in Hindi as well as English, and for most of Haryana outside corporate Gurugram, Hindi or simple Hinglish gets a better response. Some practical points:

  • Create the same template in both Hindi and English and send by customer preference.
  • Write naturally: "Namaste [name] ji, aapka order dispatch ho gaya hai" reads better than stiff translated Hindi.
  • Use variables for name, branch, amount and date so each message feels personal.
  • Add buttons: "Order karein", "Branch ko call karein", "Stop".
  • Choose the right category. Marketing, utility and authentication templates are reviewed and charged differently, and a promotional message submitted as utility can be rejected or re-categorised.

5. Cost structure in plain words

There are usually three layers of cost:

  1. Meta's charges. Meta bills business-initiated template messages by category, with marketing costing more than utility. Replies within the customer service window are generally not charged as templates. Rates and rules have changed more than once, so check Meta's current official rate card for India.
  2. Platform or provider fee. If you use a business solution provider's inbox and tools, they charge a monthly fee and sometimes a markup per message.
  3. Setup and integration. One-time work: number verification, templates, chatbot flows, CRM connection, staff training.

6. Linking chatbot and CRM

Campaigns create replies, and replies need handling. A sensible flow for a multi-city business:

  1. Customer or dealer replies to a campaign.
  2. A chatbot asks one or two questions: which city or branch, what they need.
  3. The chat is routed to the right branch or the dealer's assigned salesperson.
  4. The lead and conversation are saved in the CRM with source and campaign name.
  5. The owner sees branch-wise numbers: messages sent, replies, orders.

Without this link, WhatsApp marketing becomes noise. With it, you know which branch and which campaign earned money. See how a custom CRM or an AI chatbot fits in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is bulk WhatsApp marketing legal and safe?
Sending through the official WhatsApp Business API to people who have opted in is allowed. Using unofficial bulk sender tools on purchased number lists breaks WhatsApp's terms and commonly gets numbers banned, so we do not offer it.
Q: How much does WhatsApp marketing cost in Haryana?
Cost has three parts: Meta's per-message charges by category, any provider platform fee, and one-time setup. Inovix has no fixed published price; a scope-based quote is shared before work starts. Check Meta's current official rates, as they change.
Q: Can several branches use one WhatsApp number?
Yes. With the WhatsApp Business API, many staff across branches can work from one number through a shared inbox, with chats routed to the right branch. Some businesses prefer a separate number per city; both are possible.
Q: Can templates be sent in Hindi?
Yes. Templates can be submitted for approval in Hindi and English, and you can send each customer the language they prefer. Simple, natural Hindi or Hinglish usually works better than formal translated text.
