Website Design Cost in India 2026: Real Prices by Website Type

Website banane ka kharcha kitna hota hai? A plain-language breakdown of what Indian businesses typically pay in 2026, and what you should get for that money.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Ask five agencies about website design cost in India and you will hear five different numbers, anywhere from ₹3,000 to ₹3 lakh. All of them can be honest quotes, because they are quoting for very different things. This guide explains what a website typically costs in 2026, what actually changes the price, and which yearly charges you should ask about before paying any advance.

One note before the numbers: the market ranges below are typical estimates based on what agencies and freelancers usually quote. They are not official data. Our own prices are exact and are listed on our pricing page.

1. Website Design Cost in India by Website Type

The biggest factor is simply what kind of website you need. A five-section site for a clinic is a few days of work. An online store with payments and stock tracking is several weeks.

2. What Actually Drives the Price

Two quotes for "a website" can differ by ten times. These are the usual reasons.

  • Number of unique page designs. Ten pages that share one layout cost far less than ten pages that are each designed separately.
  • Template or custom design. A ready-made theme with your logo and photos is quick. A design made around your brand takes real design hours.
  • Content. If the agency writes your text, edits photos and makes banners, the cost goes up. If you supply everything ready, it comes down.
  • Features. Booking forms, payment gateway, login area, multiple languages, WhatsApp alerts, CRM connection. Each one adds development and testing time.
  • SEO depth. Basic setup such as titles, sitemap and Google Business Profile is one thing. Researching keywords and writing separate pages for each service and city is much more work.
  • Who builds it. A student freelancer, an experienced freelancer, a small agency and a large metro agency all have different costs to cover. Higher price does not always mean better work, but very low price usually means a template and little support.

3. Hidden Renewals Nobody Mentions

The build price is only part of the kharcha. A website has running costs every year, and many business owners discover them only when the first renewal invoice arrives.

  • Domain name: usually around ₹700 – ₹1,500 a year for .in or .com. Watch for a cheap first year followed by a higher renewal price.
  • Hosting: shared hosting typically ranges from ₹2,000 – ₹8,000 a year. First-year discounts are common, so always ask for the renewal rate.
  • SSL certificate: free with most modern hosts. If someone charges for basic SSL every year, ask why.
  • Business email: Google Workspace or similar is charged per user per month. Check the current official price.
  • Paid themes and plugins: on WordPress, premium plugins often renew yearly.
  • Maintenance or AMC: updates, backups and small edits. Our guide on website maintenance cost in India covers this in detail.

Before you sign, ask for one simple line in writing: the total cost in year one, and the total cost from year two onwards.

4. One-Time Payment vs Monthly Plans

You will see two pricing models in the market.

One-time build

You pay for the website once, then pay only hosting and domain each year. This suits most businesses because the website becomes an asset you own. Make sure the agreement says you get the source code and the domain is registered in your name.

Monthly subscription website

You pay ₹500 – ₹3,000 every month and the provider builds and hosts the site. It looks cheap at the start, but in many such plans the site disappears when you stop paying, and you cannot move it elsewhere. Over three years you can easily pay more than a one-time build. 5. How to Compare Two Quotes Fairly

A quote of ₹8,000 and a quote of ₹25,000 may both be reasonable. Put these questions to both sides and compare the answers, not just the totals.

  1. How many pages and how many rounds of changes are included?
  2. Is the design a template or made for my business?
  3. Who owns the domain, hosting account and source code?
  4. What will I pay every year after launch?
  5. Is the site fast on a normal mobile connection? Ask for live examples and test them yourself.
  6. Who do I call when something breaks, and how fast do they reply?
  7. What are the payment stages? Milestone payments protect you better than 100% advance. We work on 30% / 40% / 30%.

6. How Much Should You Actually Spend?

A simple rule: spend according to what the website has to do for you.

  • Just need to look genuine online (shop, clinic, tutor, contractor): a clean one-page or small site in the ₹5,000 – ₹15,000 band is enough. Do not overspend.
  • Want enquiries from Google (services, B2B, manufacturers, exporters): you need separate pages per service, proper SEO structure and lead capture. Budget ₹20,000 – ₹60,000.
  • Selling online or running operations on it: this is software, not a brochure. Plan the budget and timeline accordingly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the minimum cost of a business website in India in 2026?
A basic one-page business website typically costs between ₹3,000 and ₹15,000 in the Indian market. At Inovix, the Launchpad Website is ₹4,999 one-time and includes a WhatsApp lead button, Google Maps and local SEO setup, and one month of free maintenance.
Q: Website banane ka kharcha har saal kitna aata hai?
After the build, expect domain renewal of roughly ₹700 to ₹1,500 a year and hosting of roughly ₹2,000 to ₹8,000 a year, plus any maintenance plan you choose. Always ask the agency for the year-two cost in writing because first-year discounts are common.
Q: Is a monthly website plan better than a one-time payment?
For most businesses a one-time build is better because you own the website and only pay small running costs. Monthly plans are fine for a short test, but check whether you can take the site and domain with you if you stop paying.
Q: Why do website quotes differ so much between agencies?
Because the scope is different. Number of custom-designed pages, who writes the content, features like payments or booking, SEO depth, and the support included after launch all change the hours involved. Compare what is included, not only the final number.
