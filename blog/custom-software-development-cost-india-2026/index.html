Custom Software Development Cost in India (2026 Price Guide): Real Rates & Timelines

How much should you really pay to build custom business software, a SaaS web app, or an internal ERP in India? Complete honest cost matrix by Inovix.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Building custom software in India has become the top strategy for businesses looking to gain an unfair advantage over competitors. But software pricing remains notoriously confusing — one agency quotes ₹50,000 while another quotes ₹15,00,000 for what sounds like the exact same project.

In this comprehensive 2026 guide, we unpack the real costs of software engineering in India, breakdown hourly vs. milestone models, and explain how modern developer toolchains let founders launch faster for a fraction of traditional agency costs.

1. Custom Software Cost Matrix in India (2026 Benchmarks)

Software Type Typical Complexity Timeline Realistic Indian Market Cost
Internal Billing & Inventory Tool Low - Medium 2 – 3 Weeks ₹30,000 – ₹60,000 ($350 – $750)
Custom Sales CRM & Lead Tracker Medium 3 – 5 Weeks ₹45,000 – ₹95,000 ($550 – $1,200)

Enterprise Custom Platform (Multi-tenant) Very High 12+ Weeks ₹2,50,000 – ₹8,00,000+ ($3,000+)

2. What Factors Drive Software Costs Up or Down?

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Does the software only have an Admin, or do you need Manager, Accountant, Operator, and Customer portals with distinct permissions?
  • Third-Party Integrations: WhatsApp Business API, Razorpay/Stripe payments, Tally data export, and SMS gateways add integration complexity.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Traditional architectures require heavy server management. Inovix builds on serverless & edge platforms (Cloudflare, Supabase, Vercel), cutting recurring hosting costs by up to 90%.
  • Mobile Responsiveness: Native iOS/Android apps cost 2x more. Instead, progressive web applications (PWAs) work across both mobile and desktop from a single codebase, saving you lakhs.

3. Beware of Massive Agency Overheads in Delhi, Mumbai & Bangalore

Why do big city agencies charge ₹5 to 10 Lakhs for standard software? You aren't paying for better code — you are paying for their prime office rent in Cyber City Gurugram, project managers, junior account coordinators, and sales bonuses.

Working directly with an agile engineering studio like Inovix gives you direct access to senior full-stack developers with zero middlemen markup.
