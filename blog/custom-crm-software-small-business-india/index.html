CRM Software for Small Business in India: Ready-Made vs Custom (2026)

Leads in a notebook, follow-ups on WhatsApp, payments in Excel. If that sounds familiar, here is how to choose a CRM without overpaying.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Most owners start searching for CRM software for small business in India after the same painful moment: a good lead called twice, nobody followed up, and the order went to a competitor. A CRM fixes that by putting every lead, follow-up and deal in one place. The real question is whether to rent a ready-made CRM or build a custom one. Both are valid. This guide helps you choose with numbers, not opinions.

1. What a CRM Actually Does for a Small Business

Forget the jargon. For a team of 5 to 30 people, a CRM should do four jobs:

  • Capture every enquiry from website, WhatsApp, calls, IndiaMART, Facebook and walk-ins into one list.
  • Assign each lead to a person, so nothing is "everybody's job".
  • Remind that person to follow up, and show the owner what is pending.
  • Report which source brings real orders and which salesperson closes them.

If a tool does these four well and your team uses it every day, it is a good CRM for you. Fancy features that nobody opens are wasted money.

2. Ready-Made SaaS CRM: Where It Wins

Products like Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Freshsales, Bitrix24 and several Indian lead-management tools are mature and reliable. Choose a ready-made CRM when:

  • Your sales process is standard: lead, follow-up, quote, won or lost.
  • You have 1 to 5 users. Many tools have a free or low-cost tier for small teams.
  • You want to start this week, not next month.
  • You are not yet sure what you need. Renting for three months is the cheapest way to find out.

The honest downsides: the price is per user per month and grows with your team, the features you need are often locked in higher plans, and you adjust your process to the software instead of the other way round.

3. The Per-User Rental Math

SaaS CRM pricing looks small because it is shown per user per month. Multiply it out before deciding. Paid plans of popular CRMs typically fall somewhere between ₹800 and ₹4,000 per user per month depending on product and tier. Prices change often, so check the current official price. Add-ons such as WhatsApp integration, extra storage, telephony and automation credits are usually billed separately. For 3 users, renting is clearly sensible. Somewhere around 8 to 15 users, the three-year rental often crosses what a custom build would cost. That is the point to compare seriously.

4. Custom CRM Development Cost in India

A custom CRM is built around how your business actually works. You pay once for development, then a small amount for hosting and maintenance. There is no per-user fee, so adding your 20th employee costs nothing extra.

Typical 2026 market ranges:

  • Basic lead and follow-up CRM (leads, pipeline, reminders, simple reports): ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000
  • CRM with quotations, billing, inventory or staff dashboards: ₹1,50,000 – ₹5,00,000
  • Industry-specific system with multiple branches, approvals and integrations: ₹5,00,000 and above

Custom has honest downsides too. It takes weeks, not minutes. You depend on the quality of the developer. And if your requirement is vague, the project will drift. Custom works best when you already know your process well.

5. Must-Have Features (and What to Skip)

Must have

  • Lead capture from website forms and WhatsApp, with source tagging
  • Duplicate check by phone number
  • Follow-up reminders with a daily "today's calls" list
  • Pipeline stages you can rename to match your business
  • Role-based access, so staff see only their leads and the owner sees everything
  • Mobile-friendly screens, because your sales team is not sitting at a desk
  • Export to Excel and regular backups
  • Simple reports: leads by source, conversion by person, pending follow-ups

Usually safe to skip in year one

  • AI lead scoring
  • Complex marketing automation journeys
  • Dozens of dashboards nobody reads

6. WhatsApp Integration: What Is Possible

In India, sales happen on WhatsApp. A CRM that ignores it will not be used. There are two levels:

  • Click-to-chat: a button in the CRM opens WhatsApp with the customer's number and a ready message. Simple, free, works with the normal WhatsApp Business app.
  • WhatsApp Business API: the official route for automated messages such as instant replies to new leads, quote and payment reminders, order status and chatbots. It needs a verified business, approved message templates, and Meta charges per message or conversation depending on category. Check Meta's current pricing.

Be careful with tools that send bulk messages through unofficial methods. Numbers get banned, and you lose your main sales channel overnight.

7. A Simple Decision Guide

  • 1–5 users, standard sales process: rent a SaaS CRM. Start on a free or basic plan.
  • 5–15 users: do the three-year math above. If you also need billing, inventory or WhatsApp automation in the same system, custom starts to look better.
  • 15+ users, or an unusual process (dealers, job work, site visits, multi-step approvals): custom usually pays back, and you own the software.
  • Not sure: rent for three months, write down everything that irritates you, and use that list as the specification for a custom build.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which CRM is best for a small business in India?
For teams of up to five people with a standard sales process, a ready-made CRM such as Zoho CRM, HubSpot or Freshsales on a free or basic plan is usually the best start. Larger teams or businesses with unusual workflows often get better value from a custom CRM.
Q: What is the custom CRM development cost in India?
A basic custom CRM with leads, pipeline and reminders typically costs ₹50,000 to ₹1,50,000. Systems with billing, inventory and staff dashboards usually range from ₹1.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh. Inovix custom software and CRM projects start at ₹49,999 with full source-code handoff.
Q: Can a CRM be connected to WhatsApp?
Yes. The simple way is a click-to-chat button that opens WhatsApp with a ready message. For automated replies, reminders and chatbots you need the official WhatsApp Business API, which requires business verification and has per-message charges set by Meta.
Q: Is a custom CRM cheaper than a SaaS CRM in the long run?
It depends on team size. For three users, renting is cheaper. For ten or more users on a paid plan, three years of per-user fees often exceed the one-time cost of a custom CRM, which has no per-user charge. Do the math for your own team before deciding.
