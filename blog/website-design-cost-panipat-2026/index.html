2. Beware: The 4 Hidden Charges Cheap Agencies Never Tell You

Many agencies in Haryana offer a "₹3,000 website" to hook you in. But by month 12, your bill easily crosses ₹15,000 due to hidden traps:

  • Trap 1: Expensive Year 2 Hosting & Domain Renewal: They buy a ₹400 domain, register it in their own company name (not yours!), and then demand ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 next year to keep your website live.
  • Trap 2: Paid Theme & Plugin Licenses: Cheap WordPress sites use nulled/pirated themes. When the developer leaves, the theme breaks or gets infected by malware, requiring ₹6,000+ in emergency repairs.
  • Trap 3: Content Update Fees: Want to change a phone number or add 2 new photos? They charge ₹1,000 per minor edit.
  • Trap 4: Domain Hostage: If you try to switch to another developer, they refuse to release your domain name until you pay a heavy "release penalty".
