Mobile App Development Cost in India 2026: Native, Hybrid and PWA Prices

An app can cost ₹40,000 or ₹40 lakh. The difference is the approach, the features and the backend. Here is how to estimate yours.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

The honest answer to "what is the mobile app development cost in India?" is that it depends on three choices: how the app is built (native, cross-platform or PWA), what it does, and how much backend it needs. This guide gives typical 2026 ranges for each, so you can walk into any agency meeting with a realistic number in mind.

All market ranges here are estimates of what Indian agencies and freelancers usually quote. They are not verified statistics. Store fees are set by Google and Apple and can change, so check the official pages for current amounts.

1. Native vs Cross-Platform vs PWA

This single decision can halve or double your budget.

ApproachWhat it meansTypical cost for a mid-size appBest when
NativeSeparate Android (Kotlin) and iOS (Swift) apps₹4,00,000 – ₹20,00,000+ for bothHeavy camera, Bluetooth, games, top performance
Cross-platformOne codebase (React Native or Flutter) for both stores₹2,00,000 – ₹10,00,000Most business apps
PWAA web app that installs from the browser and works like an app₹50,000 – ₹4,00,000Internal tools, ordering, booking, tight budgets

A word on PWAs

A Progressive Web App runs in the browser but can be added to the home screen, open full-screen and work with a weak connection. There is no app store approval and one build works on Android, iPhone and desktop. The limits: it is not listed in the stores by default, and some device features and iPhone notification behaviour are more restricted than in native apps. For staff dashboards, order-taking and customer portals, a PWA is very often enough.

2. Mobile App Development Cost in India by App Type

These ranges assume a cross-platform build with a proper backend and admin panel.

  • Simple informational or catalogue app (few screens, no login): ₹40,000 – ₹1,50,000
  • Booking or appointment app (login, calendar, notifications, payments): ₹1,50,000 – ₹5,00,000
  • Ecommerce app (catalogue, cart, payments, order tracking): ₹2,00,000 – ₹8,00,000
  • Field staff or business operations app (attendance, orders, reports, roles): ₹1,50,000 – ₹6,00,000
  • On-demand or marketplace app (customer app + partner app + admin panel, live tracking): ₹6,00,000 – ₹25,00,000+
  • Social, chat or streaming app: ₹8,00,000 and upwards, with significant server costs

A marketplace is expensive because it is really three products: a customer app, a partner app and an admin system. When someone quotes ₹50,000 for "an app like Zomato", they are quoting for a template, not for a working business.

3. What Pushes the Cost Up

  • Backend and admin panel. The screens you see are roughly half the work. The server, database and admin dashboard are the other half.
  • Number of user roles. Customer, vendor, delivery, manager, admin. Each role means separate screens and permissions.
  • Real-time features. Live location, chat, live order status.
  • Third-party services. Payment gateway, SMS and OTP, maps, WhatsApp Business API, push notifications. Many of these have their own usage charges.
  • Custom UI and animation. Standard components are quick. Fully custom interfaces take design and development time.
  • Offline mode and sync. Very useful for field staff, and tricky to build correctly.

4. App Store Fees and Running Costs

These are separate from development and are paid by you directly.

  • Google Play developer account: a one-time registration fee, around 25 US dollars at the time of writing.
  • Apple Developer Program: an annual fee, around 99 US dollars a year. Stop paying and the app leaves the App Store.
  • Store commission: both stores take a percentage on digital goods and subscriptions sold inside the app. Physical products and services paid through a normal gateway are generally outside this, but read the current policies.
  • Server and database: from a few hundred rupees a month for a small app to much more as users grow.
  • SMS and OTP: charged per message. Small per unit, noticeable at volume.
  • Maps and notifications: often free up to a usage limit, then paid.

Create the store accounts in your own company name. If the agency publishes the app under their account, moving it later is a headache.

5. Maintenance: The Yearly Cost Nobody Budgets

Android and iOS release new versions every year. Stores keep raising their minimum requirements, and apps that are not updated can be hidden or removed. Libraries need security updates. Users report bugs on phones you have never heard of.

A common rule of thumb in the industry is to budget roughly 15% – 20% of the build cost per year for maintenance. So a ₹4 lakh app typically needs ₹60,000 – ₹80,000 a year to stay healthy. Ask every agency what their post-launch support includes and what it costs after the free period ends.

6. Do You Need an App at All?

Many small businesses ask for an app when a fast mobile website or PWA would do the job at a fraction of the price. A quick test:

  • Will the same customer use it several times a month? If not, they will not keep it installed.
  • Do you need device features like background location, Bluetooth or heavy offline use? If not, the web can probably handle it.
  • Do you already have customers asking for it, or is it a guess?

Our guide on website vs web app vs mobile app walks through this decision with examples. A sensible path for most businesses is: start with a web app or PWA, prove that people use it, then invest in store apps.

7. How to Get a Reliable Quote

  1. Write down every user role and what each one must be able to do.
  2. Mark features as "must have for launch" and "later". Launch small.
  3. Ask for a milestone-wise plan with payments tied to working demos, not dates.
  4. Confirm in writing that you get the full source code and that store accounts are in your name.
  5. Ask what happens after launch: bug-fix period, maintenance cost, response time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the minimum cost to build a mobile app in India?
A simple informational or catalogue app typically starts around ₹40,000 to ₹1,50,000 in the Indian market. A Progressive Web App can be cheaper for the same features. Apps with login, payments and an admin panel usually start from ₹1.5 lakh and go up with complexity.
Q: Is Flutter or React Native cheaper than native app development?
Usually yes. Cross-platform frameworks use one codebase for Android and iOS, which typically costs 30 to 50 percent less than building two separate native apps. Native still makes sense for games and apps that depend heavily on device hardware.
Q: How much are Google Play and Apple App Store fees?
Google Play charges a one-time developer registration fee of around 25 US dollars, and Apple charges around 99 US dollars per year. Both also take a commission on digital goods sold in the app. These amounts can change, so check the official developer pages.
Q: How much does app maintenance cost per year?
A common rule of thumb is 15 to 20 percent of the original build cost per year. This covers OS updates, store requirement changes, security patches and bug fixes. Server, SMS and other usage-based charges are separate.
