Custom GST Billing and Inventory Software for Shops: Cost, Features & Who Needs It (2026)

Most shops do fine with ready-made billing software. Some genuinely need custom. Here is how to know which side you are on before spending money.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Search for custom GST billing and inventory software for shops and every developer will tell you that you need custom software. We will start with the opposite: most small shops do not. A good ready-made billing app is enough for a single counter with standard products. Custom software makes sense only in specific situations — and when it does, it can save a lot of time, stock loss and daily headache. This guide helps you decide honestly.

1. What billing and inventory software must do in any shop

Whether ready-made or custom, the basics are the same:

  • GST invoices with GSTIN, HSN/SAC codes and correct CGST, SGST or IGST split
  • Fast billing with barcode scanning or quick item search
  • Stock that reduces automatically with each sale and increases with each purchase
  • Purchase entry and supplier ledger
  • Customer ledger and udhaar (credit) tracking with reminders
  • Low-stock alerts
  • Sales, purchase and tax reports your accountant can use for GST returns
  • Bill printing on thermal or A4 printers, and bill sharing on WhatsApp
  • Regular data backup

If a software does these well, you have covered what most shops need.

3. Who should stay with ready-made software

  • A single shop with one or two billing counters
  • Standard products with simple pricing — kirana, stationery, mobile accessories, a small medical store
  • Owners who mainly need GST bills, stock count and basic reports
  • Businesses whose process may change a lot in the next year

For these, a ready-made app at a few thousand rupees a year is the sensible choice. Spending ₹50,000 or more on custom software would be a waste, and we will tell you so if you ask us.

4. Who actually needs custom software

Custom starts paying off when your business has rules that ready-made products cannot handle properly:

Multiple branches or godowns

You want one live view of stock across locations, stock transfers between them, and branch-wise profit — without paying for a separate licence at each counter.

Unusual units and pricing

Cloth sold by metre and by than, hardware sold by piece and by weight, items with size-colour variants, dealer-wise or quantity-wise rate lists, schemes and commissions.

Wholesale with dealers and salesmen

Salesmen book orders from the field on mobile, dealers check their ledger and place orders themselves, and billing happens from those orders without retyping.

Manufacturing plus trading

Raw material goes in, finished goods come out, job work is sent outside. Billing must connect with production. For textile units we have a dedicated guide: textile factory ERP and billing software.

Owner control and staff permissions

The owner wants a mobile dashboard, and wants to control exactly who can give discounts, edit bills or see purchase rates.

Connection with other systems

Your billing should talk to your online store, send automatic WhatsApp bills and payment reminders, or feed a custom report format your CA or head office wants.

If two or more of these match your business, custom is worth a serious look.
