AI Agent Development Company in Panipat: Real Use Cases, Limits and Costs

An AI agent is not a robot employee. It is software that reads, decides and takes one small action at a time, and it works best on boring, repeated tasks.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Every business owner has now heard that "AI will do everything". Then they try a chatbot, it gives a wrong price to a customer, and the whole idea is dropped. The truth sits in the middle. A well-scoped AI agent can take real load off your team, and a badly scoped one creates new problems. If you are searching for an AI agent development company in Panipat, this guide explains in plain words what you would be buying, where it helps and where it should not be trusted.

1. AI agent kya hota hai? A plain-words explanation

A normal chatbot follows a fixed menu: press 1 for price, press 2 for address. An AI agent is different in three ways:

  • It understands free text. A customer can write "bhai 200 pc double bed blanket ka rate bata do, delivery Surat" and the agent understands product, quantity and destination.
  • It uses your data. It looks up your price list, stock sheet, order status or appointment calendar instead of guessing.
  • It can take an action. It can draft a quotation, create a lead in your CRM, book a slot or send a reminder, within limits you define.

Technically, it is a large language model (accessed through an LLM API) connected to your systems through APIs and webhooks, with written rules about what it may and may not do. Think of it as a trained junior assistant who never sleeps but must always follow the rulebook.

3. The limits you must know before you buy

We would rather tell you this now than after a bad experience.

  • AI can be confidently wrong. Language models sometimes invent answers. That is why prices, stock and policies must come from your data, not from the model's memory, and why quotations should be approved by a person.
  • It is only as good as your records. If the rate list is in someone's head or in five different Excel files, the first job is cleaning that up.
  • Not for medical, legal or financial advice. A clinic's agent can book and remind. It must not diagnose.
  • Hindi and Hinglish work well, but not perfectly. Voice notes, heavy local slang and blurry photos still confuse it. The agent should say "connecting you to our team" instead of guessing.
  • Running costs exist. LLM providers charge by usage, and WhatsApp charges for business-initiated messages. Both change, so check the current official pricing pages before budgeting.

4. Human handoff: the part most projects skip

A safe agent knows when to stop. In every build we define handoff rules in writing:

  1. The customer asks for a person, or types the same question twice.
  2. The agent's answer is not found in the approved knowledge.
  3. The message involves a complaint, a refund, a discount request or an amount above a set limit.
  4. The conversation is with an existing key account that the owner wants to handle personally.

On handoff, the full chat goes to the right staff member with a short summary, so the customer does not have to repeat everything. Every conversation is logged so you can read what the agent said in your name.

5. How we work as an AI agent development company in Panipat

  1. One job, not ten. On a WhatsApp call with the founder, Sagar Punia, we pick the single most repeated task. A narrow agent that works beats a broad one that embarrasses you.
  2. Collect the knowledge. Price lists, FAQs, policies and sample chats from your own team.
  3. Build and connect. We use Node or Python with LLM APIs, Supabase for data, and the official WhatsApp Business API, website chat or email as the channel.
  4. Test with real questions. Your staff try to break it. We tighten the rules wherever it slips.
  5. Soft launch. For the first weeks the agent drafts and a human sends. Once you trust it, simple replies go automatic.
  6. Handover. You receive the source code, and the API accounts and WhatsApp number stay in your name.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?
A basic chatbot follows fixed menus and scripted replies. An AI agent understands free-text messages, looks up your own data such as price lists or order records, and can take a limited action like drafting a quotation or booking a slot, under rules you define.
Q: Can an AI agent reply to customers on WhatsApp in Hindi?
Yes. Modern language models handle Hindi and Hinglish well for common questions. It should run on the official WhatsApp Business API, and unclear messages such as voice notes or heavy slang should be handed to a human instead of guessed.
Q: How much does an AI agent cost for a small business?
Inovix gives a scope-based quote on WhatsApp before work starts. As a market estimate, simple FAQ agents on ready platforms cost a few thousand rupees to set up, while custom agents connected to your software are projects in the range of custom software, plus usage charges from the AI and WhatsApp providers.
Q: Will an AI agent replace my staff?
Realistically no. It removes repeated first-level work like answering the same questions and chasing follow-ups, so your team can spend time on negotiation, relationships and problems that need judgement.
