Website Maintenance Cost in India 2026: AMC Charges and What They Cover

A website is not a one-time purchase like a signboard. Here is what it needs every year, what that should cost, and what you can safely do yourself.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

Most business owners find out about website maintenance cost in India the hard way: a renewal reminder they did not expect, a site that suddenly shows a security warning, or a contact form that silently stopped sending enquiries three months ago. A website needs a small amount of regular care, just like a vehicle needs servicing. This guide explains what that care includes, what agencies typically charge for it, and how to avoid paying for things you do not need.

The price ranges below are typical market estimates for 2026, not official data. Hosting and domain prices vary by provider, so always check the current rate.

1. What Does a Website AMC Actually Cover?

AMC means Annual Maintenance Contract. You pay a fixed amount per month or year, and the agency keeps the website healthy. A proper AMC should cover most of these:

  • Hosting and uptime: the site stays online and someone notices if it goes down.
  • Domain and SSL renewals: tracked and renewed on time, so the site does not vanish over a missed email.
  • Backups: regular copies of the site that can actually be restored.
  • Software updates: CMS, theme, plugin and library updates, especially important on WordPress.
  • Security: monitoring, malware cleanup and fixing vulnerabilities.
  • Bug fixes: broken forms, layout issues after a browser update, dead links.
  • Small content edits: changing a phone number, price, photo or banner. Usually limited to a number of changes or hours per month.
  • Speed and form checks: making sure pages still load fast and enquiries still reach you.

What an AMC normally does not cover: new pages, redesigns, new features, SEO campaigns, ad management and content writing. These are separate work. Ask for the inclusion list in writing so there is no argument later.

3. Why the Charges Differ So Much

  • Technology. A static or React site has very little to update. A WordPress site with twenty plugins needs attention every month. An online store with payments needs it every week.
  • Traffic and hosting. More visitors and more data need better servers.
  • How many edits you request. A site that changes twice a year costs less to look after than one with daily updates.
  • Response time. "We will fix it within 3 working days" is cheaper than "we respond within 2 hours".
  • Risk. If the site takes payments or holds customer data, security work is more serious.

4. The Renewals That Are Separate From AMC

Some yearly costs exist whether or not you have a maintenance contract:

  • Domain: usually around ₹700 – ₹1,500 a year for .in or .com.
  • Hosting: if not included in the AMC, shared hosting typically costs ₹2,000 – ₹8,000 a year. Renewal prices are often higher than first-year offers.
  • Business email: per user, per month.
  • Premium plugins and themes: yearly licences on WordPress.

One important rule: the domain must be registered in your name with your email ID. If the agency holds it, you cannot change vendors without their cooperation. We have covered these hidden charges in more detail in website design cost in India.

5. DIY vs Managed Maintenance

When doing it yourself is fine

  • Your site is a simple static or React site that rarely changes.
  • You or someone on your team is comfortable with hosting dashboards.
  • You are disciplined enough to set renewal reminders and test your contact form every month.

A simple DIY checklist

  1. Put domain and hosting expiry dates in your phone calendar, with a reminder 30 days before.
  2. Once a month, open the site on your mobile and submit the enquiry form yourself.
  3. On WordPress, update plugins monthly and take a backup before each update.
  4. Use a strong, unique admin password and remove old user accounts.
  5. Download a full backup every quarter and keep a copy off the server.

When managed care is worth paying for

  • Your website brings leads or sales, so every day of downtime costs money.
  • You run WordPress or an online store and do not want to handle updates.
  • You simply do not have the time. For most owners, this is the real reason.

The technology you choose affects this a lot. See WordPress vs React website for how upkeep differs between the two.

6. Red Flags in an AMC Quote

  • No written list of what is included and what is extra.
  • The domain or hosting account is in the agency's name and they will not transfer it.
  • Yearly "SSL charges" for a basic certificate that most hosts give free.
  • No mention of backups, or backups stored only on the same server as the site.
  • A long lock-in period with a penalty for leaving.
  • No defined response time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the average website maintenance cost in India?
For a small business website, maintenance typically ranges from ₹300 to ₹1,500 per month. WordPress sites usually cost ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 per month, and ecommerce stores ₹3,000 to ₹15,000 per month. These are market estimates and depend on what the plan includes.
Q: What is included in website AMC charges?
A proper AMC usually covers hosting or uptime monitoring, domain and SSL renewal tracking, backups, software and security updates, bug fixes and a limited number of small content edits. New pages, redesigns, new features and SEO campaigns are normally charged separately.
Q: Can I maintain my website myself without an AMC?
Yes, if it is a simple site that rarely changes. Set reminders for domain and hosting renewals, test your enquiry form every month, keep backups and update software regularly. For WordPress sites, online stores and sites that bring regular leads, managed care is usually safer.
