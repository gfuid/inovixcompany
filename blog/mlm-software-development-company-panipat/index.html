MLM Software Development in Panipat: Plans, Features, Cost & Legal Rules (2026)

What direct-selling software really contains, what it costs to build, and why we only work with businesses that follow the Direct Selling Rules, 2021.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

If you are planning a direct-selling business and looking for MLM software development in Panipat, you need two things to be right from day one: the commission calculation and the law. A wrong formula means wrong payouts and angry distributors. A wrong business model can mean a police case. This guide covers both — the plans, the features, the typical 2026 cost, and the legal line that we at Inovix do not cross.

1. What MLM software actually does

MLM or direct-selling software is the back office of a network business. It registers distributors, records every product sale, places each person in the correct position in the network, calculates commission as per your compensation plan and tracks what has been paid. Without software, this is impossible to manage beyond a few dozen members, because every new sale can affect the income of many people above it.

2. Compensation plans: binary, matrix and unilevel

The plan decides how the network grows and how commission flows. The three most common structures are:

PlanHow it worksSoftware complexity
BinaryEach distributor has two legs (left and right). Commission is usually paid on matched sales volume of the two legs.High — needs pairing logic, carry-forward and capping rules
Matrix (forced matrix)Fixed width and depth, for example 3 x 7. When a level is full, new members spill over to the next available position.Medium to high — spillover placement must be exact
UnilevelNo width limit. Everyone you sponsor directly is on your first level; commission is paid on product sales down a fixed number of levels.Lower — simplest to build and to explain

Some companies use a hybrid, with rank bonuses or repurchase income on top. Whatever you choose, write the plan on paper with examples first. If you cannot explain a payout with a calculator, the software cannot calculate it either.

3. Core features to expect

Genealogy tree

A visual tree showing each distributor's downline, with sponsor, placement, rank and sales volume. It should load quickly even with thousands of members and work on a mobile phone.

E-wallet

Every distributor gets a wallet where earned commission is credited. They can see each entry — which sale, which level, how much — and raise a withdrawal request.

Payout management

Admin approves withdrawals, applies TDS and any admin charges as per your policy, and exports a bank payout sheet. Every payout has a record that can be audited later.

Product, order and repurchase management

Product catalogue with MRP and business volume, order placement, invoices with GST, and stock at the company or franchise level. In a legal direct-selling business, income comes from product sales — so this module is the heart of the system, not an afterthought.

Other essentials

  • KYC upload and verification (PAN, Aadhaar, bank details)
  • Distributor dashboard with income summary and team report
  • Rank and reward tracking
  • Admin reports: sales, payouts, liability, top performers
  • Role-based access for admin, accounts and support staff
  • SMS or WhatsApp alerts for orders and payouts
  • Grievance or support ticket section

4. Legal compliance: who we work with, and who we do not

This section is important, so we will be very direct. Direct selling is legal in India. Pyramid schemes and money-circulation schemes are not.

The Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules, 2021 lay down what a direct-selling company must do, and they prohibit direct-selling entities from promoting a pyramid scheme or a money-circulation scheme. Money-circulation schemes are separately banned under the Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act, 1978.

In simple terms, a compliant business looks like this:

  • Income comes from sale of real products or services to consumers, not from recruiting people.
  • No entry fee or joining fee is charged just for the right to participate.
  • Distributors are not forced to buy stock, and there is a return or buy-back policy.
  • The company is a properly registered entity in India with an office, and has written contracts with its direct sellers.
  • There is a working grievance-redressal system and KYC of direct sellers.
  • No promise of fixed or guaranteed returns.

Inovix builds software only for direct-selling businesses that follow these rules. We do not build systems for pyramid schemes, money-doubling plans, "investment with daily return" plans, crypto MLM schemes or any model where payouts come mainly from new joining money. If a plan looks like that, we will say no — whatever the budget.

We are software developers, not lawyers. Before launch, please get your compensation plan and documents checked by a qualified legal professional who understands the Direct Selling Rules. We are happy to build what your legal adviser approves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is MLM software legal in India?
The software itself is only a tool. Direct selling based on real product sales is legal and governed by the Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules, 2021. Pyramid and money-circulation schemes are prohibited. Get your plan checked by a legal professional.
Q: Which plan is best: binary, matrix or unilevel?
There is no single best plan. Unilevel is simplest to explain and build, binary rewards balanced team building, and matrix limits width and depth. Choose the one your product margins can support and your distributors can understand.
