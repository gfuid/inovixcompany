Real Estate Website Development Company: Property Dealer Website & Lead CRM (2026)

Portals sell the same lead to five dealers. Your own website and a simple CRM give you enquiries that belong only to you, and make sure none are forgotten.
Sagar Punia
Founder & Senior Web Engineer at Inovix Technologies • Panipat & NCR

If you are a property dealer or builder searching for a real estate website development company, you probably have the same problem as most of the market: leads come from portals, Facebook, hoardings and references, they sit in different phones and diaries, and half of them never get a second call. This guide explains what a good property dealer website and lead CRM should do, how they work together, and what they typically cost in 2026.

1. Why property dealers need their own website

Property portals are useful, but the lead you pay for is usually shared with other dealers, and the buyer gets ten calls in one hour. Your own website works differently:

  • An enquiry on your site comes only to you.
  • Buyers check you on Google before sending a token amount. A professional site builds trust.
  • Your listings, photos and RERA details stay in one place that you control.
  • Every Google or Facebook ad you run has a proper page to land on, not just a phone number.

In cities like Panipat, Karnal, Sonipat, Gurugram and the Chandigarh Tricity, buyers and NRIs often shortlist online first and visit later. If you are not visible online, you are not in that shortlist.

2. What a real estate website development company should build for you

Do not accept a five-page template with stock photos. Ask for these things:

  • Property listings with filters: location, budget, type (plot, flat, floor, shop, SCO), size and status.
  • Individual property pages with photos, map location, price or "price on request", and a clear enquiry button on every page.
  • Easy admin panel to add a property, change price, or mark it sold from your phone.
  • 1-click WhatsApp button that opens a chat with the property name already typed.
  • Project landing pages for each new launch, used for ads.
  • Local SEO pages such as "plots in Sector 18 Panipat" that match what buyers type.
  • Fast loading on mobile, because heavy photo galleries often make property sites slow.

3. Enquiry capture: where most property websites fail

A visitor who likes a property will not hunt for your contact page. Capture must be easy at every step:

  • Short form: name, phone, requirement. Three fields are enough.
  • "Get price and layout on WhatsApp" works better than a plain "Submit".
  • Click-to-call button fixed at the bottom on mobile.
  • Each enquiry should record which property and which ad it came from.

The second part is speed. A buyer who gets a reply in five minutes is far more likely to talk than one called the next day. So every enquiry should trigger an instant WhatsApp or SMS alert to you or your sales person.

4. Lead CRM: from enquiry to site visit to booking

A CRM is simply a shared, organised list of every lead and what happened with it. For real estate, the journey usually looks like this:

StageWhat the CRM does
New enquiryAuto-saves the lead from website, ads or manual entry and alerts the assigned person
First callRecords budget, location preference and notes
Site visit scheduledSets date and time, sends a reminder to staff and buyer
Site visit doneCaptures feedback and next follow-up date
NegotiationTracks offers and pending documents
Booked or lostMarks the result and reason, for reports

The most valuable screen is "Today's follow-ups". Property decisions take weeks or months. The dealer who calls back on the promised date usually wins, and a CRM makes sure that date is never forgotten.

Features that matter for dealers

  • Lead source tracking, so you know which portal or ad actually gives bookings.
  • Role-based access: an agent sees only his own leads, the owner sees all.
  • Requirement matching: when a new 200-yard plot comes in, see which old leads wanted exactly that.
  • Simple reports: leads, visits and bookings per agent per month.

5. WhatsApp alerts and follow-up

Buyers in India prefer WhatsApp for brochures, location pins and layout plans. A good setup can:

  • Send the owner an instant alert for every new enquiry.
  • Send the buyer the brochure and location automatically after they enquire.
  • Send site-visit reminders a day before.

Automatic messages at scale need the official WhatsApp Business API, where Meta charges a small per-message fee for template messages. Rates change, so check the current official pricing. Also message only people who have enquired or given consent; bulk messaging to bought databases can get your number blocked.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. A proper listing website includes an admin panel where you or your staff can add properties, upload photos, change prices and mark units as sold without calling the developer.
